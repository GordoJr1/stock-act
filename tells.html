How a tell is scored

Filers whose official buys preceded fast spikes — and what those same hands are buying now. A tell is an official listed-stock buy placed within 8% of the prior 20-session low that then rose at least 20% within 15 sessions. Options, bonds, and ETFs are out. Filers need a handful of scored buys and more than a one-off bounce. The board then shows recent buys by those same people, flagging any that have already run.

Not a recommendation. A March 2026 market bounce can mint a lot of “hits.” Past spikes do not mean the next ticket works. Regenerated on every site update.
